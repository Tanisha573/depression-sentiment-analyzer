🧘 Calming Tips — Try These Right Now
🌬️

4-7-8 Breathing

Breathe in for 4 seconds, hold for 7, breathe out slowly for 8. Repeat 3 times. This instantly calms your nervous system.

🖐️

5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

Name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste. This brings you back to the present moment.

💧

Drink Cold Water

Splash cold water on your face or drink a cold glass slowly. It activates your body's calming reflex immediately.

✍️

Write It Down

Write exactly how you feel without judging yourself. Getting feelings out of your head and onto paper reduces their power over you.

🤗

Reach Out to Someone

Text or call one person you trust right now. You don't have to explain everything — just say "I need to talk." Connection heals.
